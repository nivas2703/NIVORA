// CONCEPT OVERVIEW · WRITING INTEGRITY SPACE

Empowering Writing Truth & Source Accuracy

CHECKRA is an AI-powered writing integrity and source verification platform designed to help students, researchers, educators, professionals, and content creators check, verify, and improve their writing in one connected workspace.

Engineered for Writing Truth & Elevation

Similarity Check

Multi-level comparison analyzing exact matches, near phrasing, and semantic overlap across open web sources.

Source Verification

Indexes live web resources to provide verifiable context, metadata, and direct citations for detected matches.

Document Analysis

Unified native ingestion of PDF, DOCX, TXT, HTML, and Markdown files with preserved layout parsing.

AI Evidence Review

Assists reviewers in evaluating matches, distinguishing valid citations and idioms from true duplication.

Workflow Architecture

01

Native Ingestion

Upload documents or paste rich text into the secure client sandbox with instant word and token parsing.

02

Multi-Vector Scan

The core engine triggers parallel n-gram shingles, web crawling indexes, and embedding distance checks.

03

Integrity Telemetry

Visual split-pane telemetry displays side-by-side matching snippets, source domain validity, and trust score.

04

Export & Improve

Generate shareable audit reports with highlighted improvements, citation suggestions, and verification badges.

// CONCEPT OVERVIEW · SHARED LIVING OS

Smart Living & Financial Harmony for Households

HOMORA is an AI-powered financial harmony platform for shared households, roommates, and co-living spaces — effortlessly coordinating contributions, expenses, and domestic agreements without confrontation.

Core Operating Capabilities

FairShare Split Engine

Custom split models (income-weighted, room-size based, or custom ratio) calculate equitable monthly balances.

Automated Ledger

Real-time shared expenditure tracker with multi-currency conversion, receipt OCR, and instant debt simplification.

Household Chore Sync

Rotational cleaning and supply schedules balanced with financial contributions for transparent equity.

Dispute-Free Mediation

Automated gentle nudges and neutral notifications eliminate interpersonal friction over money and bills.

// CONCEPT OVERVIEW · SOCIAL IMPACT & FINANCIAL SHIELD

Financial Resilience for Workers & Communities

SAYORA is a social impact fintech initiative architected to shield informal laborers, gig delivery workers, and daily wage earners against sudden income shocks, health emergencies, and catastrophic climate disruptions.

Resilience Mechanisms

Micro-Safety Pools

Decentralized peer safety buffers that pool minute daily contributions into high-resilience community lifelines.

Parametric Payouts

Automated claim triggers linked to adverse weather events, extreme heat index, or verified mobility platform outages.

Emergency Buffer OS

Zero-predatory-interest micro-drawdown facilities designed to stave off local loan shark cycles during emergencies.

Community Shield Network

Grassroots mutual aid coordination linking worker federations, community centers, and non-profit micro-funds.
